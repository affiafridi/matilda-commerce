Prices always come from the database; sale windows, option and add-on deltas, conditional option rules, bundle component choices and quantity limits are enforced. 22 unit tests and 18 database tests (brand isolation, unavailable products, missing prices, client price ignored). Discounts, delivery fee and tax are zero until 5.5 and 5.6. Contract change: `bundleChoices` is `{ bundleItemId, variationId? }` and quote lines carry snapshot fields. Step 5.4 follows.

**Step 5.4 (cart API) is done.** `apps/api/src/modules/carts`, routes under `/api/v1/cart` (brand and market from the host, never from the client): `GET` (null until the first add; never creates a cart), `POST /lines`, `PATCH /lines/:id`, `DELETE /lines/:id` and `PATCH /contact`. A guest gets a random 32-byte token in an HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax `matilda_cart` cookie (`__Host-` outside development), stored only as a SHA-256 hash, 30 days; a signed-in customer has one open cart per brand. Mutations need a same-brand Origin and, for customers, the CSRF token. The cart stores only the product and the shopper's choices; every read re-prices through the pricing engine, and a line that can no longer be bought (unpublished, price removed, quantity above a new maximum) stays in the cart with an `issues` list and is left out of the totals. The same configuration raises the quantity of its line (identical options, add-ons and bundle choices); at most 50 lines and 99 per line; an abandoned cart reopens when its owner returns. At sign-in the guest cart is merged into the customer cart (quantities added, other lines moved, the guest cart ends `MERGED`; an empty customer cart adopts it; a failure never blocks the login). One market per cart: an empty cart follows the shopper, a filled one answers 409 `CART_MARKET_MISMATCH`. The pricing engine now prices the good lines even when another line fails (checkout still refuses any error). 3 unit and 28 database tests.

**Step 5.5 (promotions and coupons) is done.** `apps/api/src/modules/promotions`: a pure engine (`promotion-engine.ts`), an evaluator that finds what may apply, and the staff API. Rules (D5-11, D5-12): a promotion applies when it is active, inside its schedule, sold in the cart's market (none chosen means every market), not used up, open to the shopper (everyone, signed-in customers, or customers with no earlier order) and under its per-customer limit. Automatic promotions apply by priority (lower number first), then at most one coupon; a promotion that cannot combine applies only while nothing else has and blocks the rest; every discount comes off what is left and never exceeds it, and is spread over the eligible lines to the minor unit. Kinds: percentage (with a maximum), fixed amount (cart currency only), free delivery (a flag for step 5.6) and free gift (a zero-price line, only while the condition holds and the gift can be sold). Scopes: whole order, products, categories (direct membership) or collections; add-ons follow their product. Staff manage promotions and coupons under `/api/v1/admin/brands/:brandId/promotions` with `promotions.view` and `promotions.manage` (audited; a promotion with coupons or use is deactivated, never deleted; a patch changes only the fields sent). Shoppers apply a code with `PUT /api/v1/cart/coupon` (422 `COUPON_INVALID` says why) and remove it with `DELETE`; the code stays on the cart and is judged again on every read. Quotes now carry discounts, `promotions.applied`, the coupon outcome and `freeDelivery`. Usage counters are only read here: they are claimed atomically when the order is created (step 5.8). 19 unit and 37 database tests.

**Step 5.6 (delivery fee, tax and signed quote) is done.** Delivery: with an area and a method chosen, the quote finds the active zone of the market that contains the area and takes that zone's charge for the method in the cart currency (`delivery-fee.ts`). A free-delivery promotion waives the fee, then the zone's free-over threshold (judged on goods after discounts); goods below the zone's minimum order are reported as `BELOW_MINIMUM` (the order step refuses it) and an area, zone or method with no charge as `NOT_SERVICEABLE`. With no choice yet, `delivery` is null and the fee is zero. The area is only ever looked up inside the brand and market. Tax (D5-03): new market columns `tax_rate_bps` (0 to 10000) and `tax_inclusive` (migration `20261128100000_market_tax`), editable through the existing market create and update API and audited; the rate defaults to zero until finance confirms the UAE VAT setting. Tax is computed per line on its amount after discount and on the delivery fee, rounded half away from zero, so line taxes always add to the total tax; inclusive prices report the contained tax without changing the total, exclusive prices add it. Signed quote (D5-01): the cart view carries `quote.signature` and `quote.expiresAt` (15 minutes), an HMAC-SHA-256 over brand, market, currency, lines, totals and the delivery choice, keyed by the new `QUOTE_SIGNING_SECRET` (required in staging and production; development uses a random per-process key). It is a convenience only: order creation (5.8) recomputes everything. 12 unit and 8 database tests.
